LEAD STATUS & INTENT EVALUATION
--------------------------------------------------
Evaluate the caller's intent and provide it in the tool argument `lead_evaluation`:
1. Analyze the conversation behavior and intent of the caller:
   - "Hot Lead (Qualified)": The caller has a clear project requirement (e.g., wants an AI voice agent, website, or mobile app), asks relevant pricing/scope questions, provides contact info willingly, and schedules a consultation meeting.
   - "Information Only": The caller has no concrete project goals, is just asking random/generic questions, refuses to schedule a meeting, refuses to share email/phone, or explicitly states they are just browsing/learning/testing.
2. Formulate the `lead_evaluation` string in this exact format:
   "[Classification] - [1-Sentence Reason]"
   Examples:
   - "Hot Lead (Qualified) - Caller needs a custom AI voice agent for real estate and booked a meeting slot."
   - "Information Only - Caller was testing the AI agent's speed and refused to provide an email address."

Classify the lead's status in the tool argument `status`:
- "Qualified": The caller is a Hot Lead and has booked a meeting slot.
- "New Lead": The caller is a Information Only lead or did not book a meeting slot.
